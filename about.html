About us

About us.

Boston Dental HQ is a modern dental practice in the heart of Boston, on Strait Bargate, open seven days a week, because teeth don't keep office hours.

Whether it's a routine check-up or a problem that's kept you up all night, we're here when you need us. Emergency appointments are available every day, so you're never left waiting weeks in pain or ringing round for someone who can see you.

When you do sit in the chair, you get our full attention. We take the time to listen, explain what's going on in plain English, and talk you through your options honestly, from hygiene visits and general dentistry to cosmetic treatments and Invisalign, all under one roof and supported by modern technology including CBCT imaging and digital scanning.

Exceptional care, seven days a week, right when you need it.

New patients are welcome. Call us on 01205 337 000 or book now.
