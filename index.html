About us

Why choose us?

Boston Dental HQ is a brand-new, purpose-built dental practice in the heart of Boston. Five modern surgeries, the latest equipment, and a simple promise: dentistry that's easy to access, honestly priced, and there when you need it.

01

Built from scratch

A state-of-the-art five-surgery practice, purpose-built with the latest equipment — not a refurbished old clinic.

02

Open 365 days

We're open every single day of the year — including weekends and bank holidays. Dental problems don't keep office hours, so neither do we.

03

Same-day emergencies

In pain? Call our 24/7 emergency line and we'll see you the same day. No waiting weeks with a toothache.

04

Transparent fees

Clear pricing published up front, explained before any treatment starts. No surprises when you get to the desk.
