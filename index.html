FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

When do you open?+

We open in Autumn 2026 at 14–16 Strait Bargate, in the heart of Boston. Book now and we'll save your place in the opening diary, so you're among the first patients seen.

How do I book an appointment?+

Fill in the booking form above and we'll be in touch to confirm your appointment. Prefer to talk? Call us on 01205 337 000.

What happens if I have a dental emergency?+

We offer same-day emergency appointments every day of the year, and our phone line is open 24/7 for emergencies. One call and you're in the chair, not on a waiting list.

Are you open on weekends?+

Yes. We're open seven days a week, including weekends and bank holidays, because teeth don't keep office hours.

How much do treatments cost?+

Our guide prices are published on our pricing page, and every treatment plan is provided in writing with itemised costs before any work begins. The quote you get is the bill you pay.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?+

Absolutely. Our team takes real pride in caring for nervous patients: unhurried appointments, everything explained in plain English, and treatment that goes at your pace, never ours.

Where do I find you?+

14–16 Strait Bargate, Boston, Lincolnshire, PE21 6LW, right in the town centre. Open in Google Maps.
